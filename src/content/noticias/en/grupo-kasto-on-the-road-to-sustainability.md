Our world faces many global challenges, and sustainable development emerged from the urgent need to study and define the impact of human activity on the environment and people’s well-being.

Using natural resources consciously and responsibly, so as not to compromise future generations, is now part of the philosophy of thousands of companies that join this great collective effort every year.

At Grupo Kasto, a business DNA focused from the start on the well-being of its employees and the development of communities, combined with management’s genuine intention to turn our corporate culture into a culture of sustainability, has resulted in our program **“Grupo Kasto on the Road to Sustainability”**.

We set out on this path in 2024, convinced that when projects are truly embraced, they are achieved organically.

Training our people in the culture of lawfulness, integrity, human rights and ethics is only the beginning. The road ahead is long and full of challenges, but we will get there because the Group walks together.
