To celebrate the Day of the Dead, the Agroindustrias La Barca team held a very special event for employees and their families for the second year in a row. Employees’ children were invited to an afternoon of games, *pan de muerto* and the movie “The Book of Life” at our facilities.

The event was full of excitement, laughter and joy: it gave the little ones a place to have fun, strengthened family bonds and let us celebrate together a tradition that is so important to our culture.

This initiative reflects the spirit of collaboration and community that we promote at Grupo Kasto.
