On April 29 at 11:30 a.m., we successfully held the First National Drill at our Central Mill facilities. The main goal was to strengthen the culture of prevention within the organization and improve our ability to respond to potential earthquakes.

The drill was led by the Safety team together with the internal brigades, who carried it out in an organized and efficient way. We want to highlight the active, committed participation of all Central Plant staff, once again showing our responsibility and preparedness in risk situations.

We will keep working to foster safe workplaces and an organizational culture focused on prevention and the protection of all our employees.
