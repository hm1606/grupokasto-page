La Piedad, Michoacán, July 9, 2020. La Piedad Mayor Alex Espinoza and Public Services Director Ramón Romero Ramírez launched the installation of a first batch of 21 new waste bins in the old area of Ciudad del Sol.

Ciudad del Sol is one of the most populated neighborhoods in the municipality, with around 90 waste bins, 21 of which were replaced in this first batch.

The new bins were made possible with the support of companies such as Grupo Kasto and Agrícola El Rosal.
