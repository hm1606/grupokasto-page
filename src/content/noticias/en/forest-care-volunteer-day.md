As part of the group’s sustainability initiatives, on Friday, November 7, employees from several divisions took part in an environmental volunteer day in natural areas across the regions where Grupo Kasto operates. The initiative was open to everyone and drew an enthusiastic response.

Grupo Kasto sites in Guadalajara, La Barca, Ciudad Obregón and La Piedad actively joined this collective effort, showing their commitment and willingness to help care for the natural environment.

In the Guadalajara metropolitan area, volunteers went to Bosque Centinela, where they worked to conserve and strengthen the local ecosystem, digging tree basins to help young trees capture water, cleaning green areas and watering the planted trees. In La Barca, Jalisco, with the support of local authorities, 103 trees were planted around the “Molino La Concepción” flour mill; in Ciudad Obregón, Sonora, 10 trees were planted around Grupo Kasto’s facilities; and in La Piedad, Michoacán, 15 trees were planted. The plantings were planned to ensure the trees’ growth and survival.

The volunteers showed great enthusiasm and commitment, making a significant contribution to the upkeep of forests and natural areas and reaffirming the sense of environmental responsibility that drives the group.

With activities like this, the organization continues to promote initiatives that strengthen sustainability, community involvement and care for the environment.
