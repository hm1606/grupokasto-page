This is how we closed our healthy-environment program: health authorities from the Jalisco Ministry of Health unveiled the certification plaque recognizing **Grupo Kasto Molinos: Guadalajara Plant as a Healthy and Safe Workplace (ENLASSE).**

The program helps companies and workplaces promote and maintain their employees’ physical and mental health, and reduce risks to their health and well-being through safe work practices, healthy work environments and responsible work organization.

The winners of the “Less Weight, More Health” challenge were also awarded. The challenge invited all employees, especially those living with overweight or obesity, diabetes or hypertension, to spend three months working toward good physical condition and a healthy weight for a healthier life.
