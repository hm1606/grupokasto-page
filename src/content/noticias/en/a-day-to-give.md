On January 20, at **Grupo Kasto’s Flour Mills Division**, we had the good fortune of partnering with [**Blooders.org**](https://blooders.org/) on a blood donation drive with a genuine intention of helping the many people who need blood or blood products.

The event took place at Grupo Kasto Molinos’ Central Plant in Zapopan, Jalisco.

The donors’ enthusiasm defined the day: their desire to help far outweighed any nerves about needles.

The drive was very well organized, on time and in full compliance with every health protocol, both for the COVID contingency and those of the blood bank, whose staff put all our employees at ease.

**More is needed.** There will always be people who need a blood donation.

There will always be room to **further promote the culture of voluntary blood donation in Mexico**, but we are on the right track, doing our bit and confident that next time we will break our own record.
