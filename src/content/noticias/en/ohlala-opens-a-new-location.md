Ohlala keeps expanding with the opening of its eighth location in Guadalajara. Now even closer to you!

The new Valle Real location is in Plaza D’Lucca, at Avenida Santa Margarita 4099, a short distance from Real Center.

The company started operating in 2010 and has been delighting customers ever since, opening locations across much of the Guadalajara metropolitan area.

Come and visit!
