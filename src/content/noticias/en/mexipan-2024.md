On July 31, Mexipan —the most important trade fair for the baking industry— opened its doors, with Grupo Kasto Molinos and Ohlala! taking part.

Ohlala! competed for the best traditional Mexican bread, presenting the classic *concha* with a French touch.

Grupo Kasto Molinos had a booth showcasing some of our retail products, such as Sabor de la Abuela and Talpita, which were sold at the venue, as well as flours for industrial customers. We also offered an interactive memory game featuring our product catalog and product demonstrations for visitors, such as decorating donuts and mille-feuille, among others.
