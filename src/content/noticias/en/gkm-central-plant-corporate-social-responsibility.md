In January, GKM Central Plant held essential training for its 2025 emergency brigade team to strengthen emergency preparedness and reinforce its commitment to the safety and well-being of its employees and the wider community.

The training, held at the Civil Protection facilities, covered first aid, evacuation, firefighting, search and rescue, and emergency communications. Beyond practical skills, the program stressed the importance of acting responsibly and in solidarity during a crisis, fostering a culture of prevention and care both inside the plant and in its surroundings.

This edition added a social-responsibility component, highlighting how emergency preparedness can have a positive impact on the local community. The program ended with a fire drill to put what was learned into practice and evaluate the team’s response and coordination.

Initiatives like this not only keep employees safe, they also reinforce Grupo Kasto’s commitment to protecting the community, as a company that cares not only about its internal operations but also about the world around it.
