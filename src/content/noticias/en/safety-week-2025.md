To strengthen the culture of prevention and workplace well-being, Grupo Kasto held Safety Week across all its divisions, with a range of engaging, educational activities that actively involved our people.

Highlights included the “Safety Fair”, the interactive game “100 Workers Said”, and “Safety Guess Who”, making learning fun. Experts from the Mexican Social Security Institute (IMSS) gave informative talks, teams took on the “Dress the Worker in Safety Gear” challenge, and hands-on fire-extinguisher practice was offered.

These initiatives across our divisions reaffirm Grupo Kasto’s commitment to the health and safety of its employees, promoting safe and responsible workplaces.
