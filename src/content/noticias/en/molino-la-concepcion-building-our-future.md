Grupo Kasto Molinos opened a new plant called “Molino La Concepción” in La Barca, Jalisco, which will produce wheat flour and by-products to supply more than 17 Mexican states.

The mill was designed for high productivity and a lower environmental impact.

It has a photovoltaic plant that captures solar radiation and turns it into electricity, and a rainwater harvesting system that purifies rainwater for general services and for the production of flour and by-products.

Molino La Concepción has cool roofs: the buildings in the complex were waterproofed in white to reduce carbon dioxide emissions and the indoor temperature caused by solar radiation.

The mill also has large agave fields as permeable green areas, allowing water to be used rationally.

Molino La Concepción is a logistical advantage for receiving wheat thanks to its location between the wheat-growing area and the region’s largest storage center, Agroindustrias La Barca (a Grupo Kasto subsidiary), optimizing its raw-material supply.
