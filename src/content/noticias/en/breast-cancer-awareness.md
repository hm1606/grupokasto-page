To mark World Breast Cancer Day, Grupo Kasto’s Human Resources and Occupational Safety departments across its divisions held several activities to encourage prevention and share information about the disease with employees.

Our divisions hosted prevention talks, testimonial talks and awareness sessions.

At Molino La Concepción, a mobile mammography unit offered free screenings to employees and people from the area. At Agroindustrias La Barca, part of our Grain Division, an internal race was held to raise awareness of the fight against breast cancer.

In this way Grupo Kasto joins Pink October to **mark the International Day Against Breast Cancer**, giving employees tools to detect the disease early.
