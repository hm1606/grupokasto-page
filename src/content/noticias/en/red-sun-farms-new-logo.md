Strong demand for healthy food should lift production for Red Sun Farms in the coming years, says Jim DiMenna, president of the marketer based in Kingsville, Ontario.

Labor availability and plant diseases could slow the industry’s growth. Even so, Red Sun Farms is investing heavily to meet demand. The company operates in Canada, the United States and Mexico.

Red Sun Farms Mexico successfully completed a 10-hectare expansion ahead of its winter harvest, according to the company’s statement.

For the 2021 winter season, Red Sun Farms Mexico plans to add 14 more hectares, the statement said.

In February, Red Sun Farms unveiled a new logo featuring its iconic butterfly while keeping the bright, bold colors that honor the brand’s history, ensuring continuity and consistency.
