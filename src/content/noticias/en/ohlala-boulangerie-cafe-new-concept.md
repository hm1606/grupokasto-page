Thanks to the warm welcome from Guadalajara’s public, Ohlala! Boulangerie Bistrot successfully opened its new Boulangerie Café concept on Avenida Bosques de San Isidro, inside Plaza Ubika, in Zapopan: Ohlala!’s ninth location in the Guadalajara metropolitan area.

To delight its guests, the fresh and welcoming “Ohlala! Boulangerie Café” offers bread, pastries, packaged products, coffee and a short quick-service menu, including baguettes, *molletes* and pizzas, among other things.

We invite you to visit this location and enjoy the space with family, on your own, as a couple or with friends.

More information on its social media: [www.facebook.com/OhlalaPanaderiaBistrot](https://www.facebook.com/OhlalaPanaderiaBistrot) and Instagram [@ohlalapanaderiagdl](https://www.instagram.com/ohlalapanaderiagdl/).

Plaza Ubika, Av. Bosques de San Isidro No. 586, Local 113, San Isidro, Zapopan, Jalisco. Hours: Monday to Friday 8:00 am to 8:00 pm, Saturday 8:00 am to 6:00 pm and Sunday 8:30 am to 6:00 pm.
