From April 24 to 29 the group held its Safety Week, with the following talks for both operations and administrative staff:

- Optometry talk (how to care for our eyes)
- Talk on psychology
- Talk on caring for the environment
- Nutrition talk
- Training: chronic degenerative diseases

During the workshops, employees received support materials, took part in different activities and had all their questions answered, so they could better understand the topics and make the most of the week.
