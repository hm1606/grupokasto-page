The GK Agricultural Expo is an annual event held since 2023 at Agroindustrias La Barca, in La Barca, Jalisco, Mexico, bringing together leading agricultural companies and growers from the region.

The Expo was created to showcase innovative products, which can be purchased at Agroindustrias La Barca and Kasavi Comercial, both part of Grupo Kasto’s Grain Division.

Through this event, Grupo Kasto aims to share options with the region’s growers, reaffirming its commitment to the future of agriculture.

Growers from several municipalities in Jalisco, Michoacán and Guanajuato attended and discovered innovative products for farming —mainly corn and wheat— from more than 25 companies that supported us this year.

Everyone who took part, whether through the booth activities, technical talks with exhibitors, the lunch or the prize raffle at the close of the event, made our 3rd edition a great success.

We thank the exhibitors, attendees and employees who made this edition possible. We will keep working to remain a forum that promotes sustainable agriculture, with the products and services that make Grupo Kasto the best choice for growers. See you at the next edition!
