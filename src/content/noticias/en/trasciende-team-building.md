On April 19 and 20 we held our annual Trasciende event, which brings together some of Grupo Kasto’s leaders and talent. During this team-building event we worked on defining and creating a new organizational culture, supported by more than 150 prior interviews with employees from every division and external stakeholders to learn their views and feelings about the group’s history.

These two days of work are already bearing fruit, starting with the definition of Grupo Kasto’s purpose: To contribute to the integral development of communities in a sustainable way, bringing happiness to the table of every home.

We will keep working to share our organizational culture with all our employees. It is a long process in which we will involve colleagues from every company.
