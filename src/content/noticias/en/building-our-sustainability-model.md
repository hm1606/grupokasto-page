For the last quarter of the year we are very excited because, as part of our “Grupo Kasto on the Road to Sustainability” program, we will be working on the materiality assessment to design our sustainability model.

Big challenges lie ahead, such as training facilitators who will help us organize and carry out the actions that stem from our sustainability strategy.

Fortunately, our people have shown great enthusiasm and are eager to join this new stage of Grupo Kasto.

We will keep working steadily to end the year with our model designed and ready to implement.
